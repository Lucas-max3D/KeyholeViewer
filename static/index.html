Power hysteresis of keyhole formation and collapse

Every keyhole opening/collapse is mapped onto the commanded power at that moment (sheet power profile along the 4 mm line, position = v·(t − laser on)). ▲ Pon: opening on a rising ramp · ▼ Poff: collapse on a falling ramp · ◁ still open at laser off / leaving the image (Poff is below this) · △ open from the start (Pon ≤ start power) · ▷ no keyhole (Pon above this) · ●/○ constant-power line with / without keyhole. Hysteresis = Pon clearly above Poff.
Caveats: (1) near threshold the keyhole forms only after an incubation time (ms), so Pon on a rising ramp overestimates the static threshold by about ramp rate × delay; (2) the thin wall heats up along the line and ramp-down lines deposit more energy early, so part of a measured ΔP can be thermal history rather than intrinsic keyhole bistability; (3) samples without a laser-gate recording assume the laser window from the sheet.
